C.2.4 
Differential Encoder 
The differential encoder (On or Off) takes care of one set of ambiguities due the error 
correction codes being transparent. 
C.2.5 
BPSK Bit Ordering 
The encoder has the ability to select whether I is the first bit or Q is the first bit in the 
symbol word grouping for compatibility with any system. For standard operation Q is the 
first bit.
